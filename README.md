## 🧭 Overview

VelocityForge is a Windows-first companion application built for players who want more granular control over their Need for Speed sessions. It is not a game; it does not replace the game; it complements it. Imagine a steering wheel that remembers your favorite cornering style, or a rear-view mirror that tells you exactly what's happening behind you — that's the spirit of this project.

The 2026 release focuses on three pillars:

1. **Precision** — every toggle, slider, and preset is mapped to a real gameplay benefit.
2. **Presence** — the interface feels like a premium dashboard, not a developer console.
3. **Patience** — the app never pushes you toward anything. It waits, offers, and adapts.

Whether you are chasing a clean lap on a midnight highway or simply exploring the city streets at your own pace, VelocityForge keeps the experience smooth, responsive, and unmistakably yours.

## 💡 Why VelocityForge Exists

Most companion utilities are built by engineers for engineers. They speak in acronyms, they hide features behind obscure toggles, and they assume you already know what a "memory offset" is. VelocityForge takes the opposite stance.

The project began as a private notebook — a set of scripts shared between three friends who wanted to keep their racing sessions consistent across different PCs. Over time, that notebook grew a face, then a voice, then a community. In 2026, it stands as a complete desktop suite with a design language of its own.

The philosophy is simple: **a companion should feel like a co-pilot, not a control panel**.

## 🎯 Responsive UI Philosophy

A responsive interface is not just about resizing windows; it's about respect. Respect for your screen real estate, your attention span, and your patience. VelocityForge's UI was rebuilt from the ground up in 2026 to honor all three.

The layout uses a fluid grid that treats every panel as a first-class citizen. On a wide display, panels spread their wings and breathe. On a compact laptop, they fold neatly into a three-tab layout that never scrolls horizontally. Buttons grow when hovered, shrink when idle, and always maintain a 44-pixel minimum touch target — just in case you're on a touchscreen Windows tablet.

Animations are subtle and never block input. The app feels alive without being loud.

## 🚀 Getting Started

The 2026 build ships as a self-contained Windows package. No external runtime installation is required, and no developer tooling is needed to use it.

1. Acquire the latest release artifact from the official release channel.
2. Verify the file signature matches the published checksum listed in the release notes.
3. Place the executable in a folder of your choice — ideally somewhere outside of system directories.
4. Launch it once to let the first-run wizard create your local profile.
5. Follow the wizard's three-step setup: language, theme, and preset defaults.
6. Optionally enable the telemetry overlay and the boot-on-login option from the settings panel.

That's it. No factory reset, no BIOS tweaking, no registry editing.

## 🧙 Configuration Walkthrough

The configuration panel is organized into five tabs, each representing a facet of the experience:

- **Profile** — your identity within the app, including avatar, display name, and session nickname.
- **Presets** — the preset library with import, export, and clone actions.
- **Overlay** — toggles for the telemetry overlay, its position, opacity, and refresh rate.
- **Appearance** — theme selection, font scaling, animation intensity.
- **Advanced** — the deeper controls, including the repair wizard and log export.

Every change is applied live. There is no "save and restart" ritual. If you don't like a change, undo it with the small rollback arrow in the corner.

## 📦 Preset Library

VelocityForge ships with a curated starter library of presets. Each preset is a small JSON document describing a configuration state. You can open them in any text editor, tweak them, and reload them without restarting the app.

The 2026 starter pack includes:

- **Nightcruise** — low overlay opacity, subtle theme, quiet mode for late sessions.
- **TimeTrial** — maximum telemetry detail, tight fonts, no animations.
- **Showcase** — cinematic overlay position, warm theme, ambient transitions.
- **Collector** — organizes session history by vehicle category for archiving.
- **Minimalist** — hides everything except the essential numbers.

You can create your own presets by pressing the plus icon in the Presets tab. Name it, describe it, and the app will remember everything about your current state.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is this a mod for the game itself?**
A: No. VelocityForge is a standalone companion that runs alongside the game. It never modifies game files.

**Q: Does it require a persistent internet connection?**
A: No. The app works fully offline. Only update checks and optional telemetry exports touch the network.

**Q: Can I use it on multiple PCs?**
A: Yes. Export your presets and import them on another machine. Your profile is portable.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Open an issue on this repository or reach out through the in-app support chat.

**Q: What is the update cadence?**
A: The 2026 channel ships a minor update every two to three weeks, with a major feature release each quarter.
